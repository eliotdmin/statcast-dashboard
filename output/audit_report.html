01

Integrity

Each check is an assertion about how the data is supposed to behave. A failure means either the data is wrong or the assumption is — and the assumption is the more likely culprit.

  • PASS

    No duplicate pitches

    0 duplicated (game_pk, at_bat, pitch)

    A day fetched twice would double-count every rate stat. ingest_log is supposed to prevent this.

  • PASS

    Count states legal

    0 rows with impossible ball/strike counts

    Savant occasionally emits a 4-ball or 3-strike row on a mid-PA event; they should be excluded from count-state work.

  • FAIL

    Regular season only

    {'R': 2793007, 'S': 50135}

    Spring training and postseason rows contaminate qualified-player leaderboards if not filtered.

  • PASS

    xwOBA non-contact convention is consistent across seasons

    {2023: 'folded-in', 2024: 'folded-in', 2025: 'folded-in', 2026: 'folded-in'}

    'folded-in' = strikeouts/walks carry an xwOBA (K=0.0, BB~0.70). 'null' = they do not. If seasons disagree, `WHERE estimated_woba_using_speedangle IS NOT NULL` means 'batted ball' in one season and 'completed PA' in another. Use type='X' for contact.

  • PASS

    Batted balls modelled by xwOBA

    0 of 482,250 non-bunt balls in play (0.00%) have no xwOBA

    These are tracking failures, not structural nulls. Above ~1% means a real gap in the ball-tracking for that period, and every rate built on xwOBA is computed on a quietly non-random subset of contact.

  • PASS

    Every database column is documented

    undocumented: none | in schema but not in db: none

    Savant adds columns mid-season and the ingest widens the table to accept them. An undocumented column is a metric nobody has decided how to treat -- add it to schema.yaml, with its applicability condition, before anything downstream consumes it.

  • PASS

    xBA/xSLG remain contact-only

    0 non-contact rows carry an xBA

    xwOBA and xBA follow DIFFERENT conventions in the same row. Averaging xBA over all PA still drops strikeouts from the numerator while the denominator counts them.

  • PASS

    plate_x sign flips by batter side

    mean plate_x: {'L': -0.145, 'R': 0.184}

    Pitchers work away from both sides, so the two means must have OPPOSITE signs. If they don't, the handedness correction in any platoon split is backwards.

  • PASS

    pfx_x sign flips by pitcher hand

    mean pfx_x: {'L': 0.395, 'R': -0.317}

    Arm-side run is opposite-signed for lefties and righties. Pooling without flipping cancels the effect to ~zero.

  • PASS

    2023: schedule complete

    all 30 teams at 162

    A cancelled game that was never made up leaves exactly two teams at 161 and names them; those are listed in SCHEDULE_EXCEPTIONS with a reason. Anything else -- one team short, or many -- is a fetch hole, not a rainout.
